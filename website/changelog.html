Changelog

Every release of NotchPal, newest first. Already have it? NotchPal tells you in the notch when a new version is out, and updates itself. New here? Download the latest version.

0.7.1 Safety update

14 November 2026

  • NotchPal only installs updates signed by its maker. The signing key lives in the security chip of his Mac and needs his Touch ID, so nobody else can send an update to your Mac.
  • If an update can't be confirmed, NotchPal doesn't install it and points you to the official download page instead.
  • Join buttons only appear for real meeting links on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Whereby and Jitsi, never for look-alikes.

0.7.0 Apple's new Mac Dynamic Island, on every Mac

12 November 2026

  • A floating island: click the notch or swipe down on it, and the island floats just below the notch like a droplet.
  • Search in the notch with ⌥ Space: apps, files, System Settings, maths as you type, quick commands, the web, Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Apple Intelligence answers right inside the notch, privately on your Mac, and Siri lights the notch with a rainbow glow while it listens.
  • Live Activities for calls in FaceTime, Zoom, Meet, Teams and WhatsApp, Voice Memos and screen recordings, with running timers.
  • A pill shows where your sound is playing when you switch to AirPods or another speaker.
  • A welcome tour for new users, and a short tour of what's new after each update.
  • A glass slider from Ultra clear to Fully tinted, and a pointer "black hole" under the notch.

0.5.0 Updates itself

24 October 2026

  • When a new version is out, click Update in the notch: NotchPal downloads it, checks it, installs it and restarts.
  • Brightness sliders for the notch and the island, and a screen brightness slider in the Music tab.
