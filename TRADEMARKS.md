# The NotchPal name and icon

The code of NotchPal is free software under the GNU GPLv3 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The GPL covers the code. It does not cover the name **NotchPal** or its icon, which belong to Mohammed Saif.

So people always know which NotchPal is the original:

- **You may** say your project is "based on NotchPal" or "a fork of NotchPal", with a link to https://github.com/MohammedSaif29/NotchPal.
- **You may not** call your copy or changed version "NotchPal", use the NotchPal icon, or present it as the official NotchPal or as made or endorsed by Mohammed Saif.
- If you share a changed version, give it a different name and icon.

The official NotchPal is only published at https://github.com/MohammedSaif29/NotchPal.
